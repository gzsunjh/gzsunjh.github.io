Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026

xiaoA Tools is used only by its owner. It does not collect, store or share any information about other people.

With the owner's permission, it uses the Google Drive scope that allows access only to files the app itself creates. It uses this access to store the owner's encrypted backups and delivery files in the owner's own Google Drive. It cannot see or change any other files.

No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

The owner can remove this access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
